GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is the website included in the monthly price?

Yes. Each package includes website creation within its listed page limit, plus the maintenance described in that package. Domain and third-party costs are separate unless your agreement says otherwise.

Do I own my website and social accounts?

Your business should retain ownership of its domain, website account, and social accounts. We work with authorized access to provide the services in your agreement.

Can I change packages later?

Package changes can be discussed and documented for a future billing period. Any changes to deliverables or pricing should be agreed to in writing.

Do you guarantee more customers or sales?

No ethical marketing provider can guarantee a specific number of leads, sales, followers, or search rankings. We focus on delivering the agreed work and reporting useful activity and results.
